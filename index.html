🧹 How to play

Your mission is simple: clean the classroom before the 45-second timer reaches zero.

1. Press Start cleanup.

2. Tap the pieces of trash that appear around the classroom.

3. Each correct tap gives you points.

4. Faster and consecutive pickups can increase your streak.

5. Answer the environmental quiz to earn a bonus.
